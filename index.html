Story summary

At some point in history, something was lost. Civilizations rose, fell, and left behind only ruins and echoes of their choices. At the center of this forgotten world stands The Tree of Sin, an ancient presence, silent and impossible to ignore. Some say it was born from the mistakes of humanity. Others believe it was always there, merely waiting for the right moment to bloom. The world around it carries the marks of those failures. Barren lands, abandoned structures, and fragments of stories that were never fully understood. Every place holds traces of a past that tried to defy its own limits… and paid the price for it. In The Tree of Sin, players are not given all the answers. Instead, they are invited to explore, observe, and interpret the signs scattered throughout the world. Every environment, every detail, and every moment of silence becomes part of a larger narrative, a story about guilt, consequence, and redemption. What truly happened to the world? And what is the true role of the tree that remains standing when everything else has fallen? Some truths are not told. They must be discovered.
